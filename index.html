Science-Based Environmental Consulting & Forensic Solutions Delivering Defensible Results and Regulatory Compliance

Environmental Forensic Consultants (EFCI) provides science-based environmental consulting, investigation, and compliance solutions designed to protect people, properties, and organizations. Our mission is to deliver cost-effective, defensible results through thorough assessments, advanced technical expertise, and proven industry practices. Our team consists of highly trained scientists, industrial hygienists, and environmental professionals with advanced degrees, including PhD and Master's level expertise. Our professionals maintain industry-recognized certifications and training through organizations such as OSHA, EPA, and IICRC, ensuring our clients receive accurate evaluations and reliable recommendations. EFCI specializes in comprehensive environmental assessments, regulatory compliance support, and expert consulting services, including: • Expert Witness and Litigation Support Services • Asbestos Survey and Assessment Services • Lead Inspection and Survey Services • Indoor Air Quality Evaluations • Microbial and Mold Investigation Services • Environmental Compliance Consulting Through scientific integrity, detailed documentation, and technical excellence, EFCI helps clients make informed decisions while addressing environmental concerns with confidence.
