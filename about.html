About Environmental Forensic Consultants

Environmental Forensic Consultants (EFCI) provides science-based environmental consulting, investigation, and compliance solutions designed to protect people, properties, and organizations.

Our mission is to provide cost-effective environmental solutions with scientifically sound and highly defensible results while maintaining environmental compliance that ensures the safety and health of all individuals.

EFCI’s team is composed of highly trained and educated environmental professionals, including PhD, Master's, and Bachelor's degree scientists, industrial hygienists, and technical specialists. Our professionals bring extensive knowledge and experience to every project and maintain industry-recognized certifications and training through organizations such as OSHA, EPA, and IICRC.

Our team provides clients with accurate evaluations, reliable recommendations, and technically defensible documentation to support environmental decision-making, regulatory compliance, and risk management.

EFCI specializes in comprehensive environmental assessments, forensic investigations, regulatory compliance support, and expert consulting services, including:

• Expert Witness / Litigation Support Services

• Asbestos Survey and Assessment Services

• Lead Survey and Assessment Services

• Indoor Air Quality Evaluations

• Microbial and Mold Investigation Services

• Environmental Compliance Consulting

Through scientific integrity, detailed documentation, and technical excellence, EFCI helps clients identify environmental concerns, reduce risk, maintain compliance, and make informed decisions with confidence.
