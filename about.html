About Environmental Forensic Consultants

Environmental Forensic Consultants (EFCI) provides science-based environmental consulting, forensic investigations, and compliance solutions designed to protect people, properties, and organizations.

Our team of highly trained scientists, industrial hygienists, and environmental professionals delivers cost-effective, defensible results through detailed assessments, advanced technical expertise, and proven industry practices.

With expertise in environmental compliance, indoor air quality, asbestos and lead assessments, microbial investigations, and litigation support services, EFCI helps clients identify risks, maintain regulatory compliance, and make informed decisions with confidence.

Our commitment to scientific integrity, accurate documentation, and professional excellence ensures every project receives the attention and expertise required to achieve reliable results.
